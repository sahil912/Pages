Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

The short version: Mixling Planet runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or store any personal data, from adults or from children. There are no accounts, no ads, no purchases and no network connection.

Who we are

Mixling Planet ("the app", "we", "us") is a puzzle game for children aged 5 to 8, available in the App Store Kids Category. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Data we collect

None. Specifically:

  • We do not have servers. The app makes no network requests of any kind.
  • We do not require an account, sign-in, name, age, email or any personal information. The age question during setup only chooses a starting difficulty and is stored on the device.
  • We do not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.
  • We do not track anyone across apps or websites.
  • There are no in-app purchases and no external links reachable by a child.

What stays on the device

The app saves the planet (a random seed and a log of what was played: mixes made, levels cleared, discoveries) and settings such as sound and haptics, in the app's private storage on the device. This is used only to show progress, the collection, the journal, the bedtime story and the grown-ups' statistics. Deleting the app deletes all of it. A grown-up can also start a fresh planet from Settings.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by not collecting data at all. Grown-up features (settings, the puzzle of the day, challenges and the bedtime story) sit behind a parental gate.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
