Common questions

My child is stuck on a level.

Tap the light bulb. It glows the two things to mix next, one step at a time. Sending a mixling that fails costs nothing: it comes back, and the trait that needs changing lights up under it.

Where are the grown-up features?

On the planet screen, tap the small gear at the top and answer the multiplication question. You will find the puzzle of the day, the challenge builder, the bedtime story, progress and settings.

My child is older and ring 1 is too easy.

In Settings (behind the gate) choose "Ring 2 (ages 7 to 8)" as the starting ring. Earlier rings stay open.

Can we start over?

Settings → "Start a new planet". This makes a fresh planet with new levels; the current mixlings, discoveries and story are gone for good, so it asks you to confirm.

Does the game need the internet?

No. It never connects to anything. Levels are generated on the device.

Sound or vibration is bothering us.

Both can be turned off in Settings. The game also follows the iOS Reduce Motion setting.
