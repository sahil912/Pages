Mix two things. Make a mixling.

A thinking game for kids aged 5 to 8, made to be played with a grown-up nearby.

Every level is new. Levels are generated and checked so they can always be solved with what is in the tray. Every mix is a surprise with its own look, name and squeak.

How it plays

  • Drag two things into the mixing pad. Something new pops out. Mixing never fails.
  • Send a mixling down the path. A river wants someone light, a cave wants someone bright, a cliff wants someone bouncy.
  • When it can't get past, exactly one trait lights up. That is the whole lesson.

What keeps kids coming back

  • Thirteen discoveries to find and keep in a journal: hot meets wet and floats away lighter, wet meets heavy and turns to sticky mud.
  • A collection of every mixling they have made, from common to legendary.
  • Something happens overnight to one of their own mixlings.

For grown-ups

  • Puzzle of the day, the same for everyone, so you can both try it.
  • Build a challenge: pick the obstacles, the game builds a level around them.
  • A bedtime story made from today's play, with your child as the hero.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no purchases, no data collection, and nothing leaves the device. Works fully offline. Read the privacy policy.
